What changed

The public "Continue with Demo" button now calls loginDemoFresh({ skipOnboarding: true }) instead of loginDemo(). Each visitor gets their own demo-fresh-*@brain.fi identity and their own freshly-seeded tenant. /api/auth/demo still exists for internal/debug use, just not behind a button.

The skipOnboarding flag is new. loginDemoFresh deliberately showed onboarding, so a straight swap would have dropped visitors into the onboarding flow instead of a populated app — the opposite of what you wanted. The flag is opt-in at the call site, so the internal behavior is preserved.

The one thing I added beyond the ask: /api/auth/demo-fresh had no rate limit — only login and register did. As the public CTA that's an unauthenticated endpoint where every tap mints a tenant and an on-chain anchor, feeding straight into the wallet backlog. It now has a dedicated limiter, default 5 per 15 min per IP, DEMO_RATE_LIMIT_MAX to override. Verified: 5 through, then 429. I put the legacy /api/auth/demo on the same budget so the ceiling can't be bypassed by calling it directly.

Two honest caveats:

Inbox renders but Needs Review is 0. That's the pre-existing upstream proposal-pipeline gap, not this change — but it's a visible soft spot in a walkthrough.
A code review flagged the shared-IP case: several people demoing from one office share an IP, and 5 per 15 min could throttle them. Raise DEMO_RATE_LIMIT_MAX before a live session.
Sequencing risk, before you publish

This makes tenant creation a public, unauthenticated path while the anchoring wallet is already underfunded with a growing backlog. The rate limit bounds it per-IP but does nothing about breadth across many IPs, and nothing reclaims tenants once created. Publishing before demo-tenant TTL exists means demo traffic spends real funds with no cleanup. You accepted that tradeoff in principle — but the limiter is a ceiling, not a fix, so it's worth deciding deliberately whether to publish now or hold until the TTL PR lands.
